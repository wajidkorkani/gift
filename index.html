Page 04 • Meri princess,my wold

happy birthday👉👈 👀 necha kuch ha apka leya…

“Happy Birthday Meri Jaan, Meri Zindagi, Meri Duniya ❤️🎂🫶🏻,
Aaj ka din mere liye sirf tumhara birthday nahi hai… Aaj woh din hai jis din meri duniya ko woh shakhs mila tha, jo aage chal kar meri duniya banne wali thi. ❤️
Faryal ❤️ shayad main lafzon mein kabhi explain na kar paun ke tum mere liye kya ho… lekin itna zaroor kehna chahta hoon ke meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat cheez tum ho. 🥹❤️ —

Tum meri wife ho, lekin mere liye sirf wife nahi ho… tum meri best friend ho, meri happiness ho, mera sukoon ho, meri har tension ke baad wali smile ho, aur meri un duaon ka jawab ho jo maine kabhi lafzon mein maangi bhi nahi thi. ❤️
Tumhari smile dekh kar mera mood automatically acha ho jata hai. Tumhari awaaz sun kar dil ko ajeeb sa sukoon milta hai. Aur tumhara mere saath hona mujhe ye feel karwata hai ke haan… main duniya ka sabse lucky insaan hoon. 🫶🏻
Kabhi kabhi sochta hoon ke maine aisa kya acha kiya tha jo Allah ne mujhe tum jaisi humsafar de di. ❤️
Tum meri zindagi ka woh chapter ho jise main kabhi khatam nahi karna chahta. Main chahta hoon tumhare saath budha hoon, tumhare saath har birthday celebrate karun, tumhari har choti si khushi ka reason banu, aur jab tum kabhi udaas ho… to duniya se pehle tumhare paas main khara hoon. ❤️‍🩹

Or Ma Apni Zindagi Ka Har Minute Har second Apka Sath Gozarna Chata ho
Apka Bagir Ak Second Be 1000 Sal Jitna Bara Lagta Hai

Allah Pak apko Duniya Ki Har khushi dy.
Ameen😘”
